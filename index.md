# 100 Days of AI

**22 September – 30 December 2026**

100 consecutive days of learning, building and applying AI to real-world problems.

The goal is not to complete another course. It is to understand how modern AI systems work by building practical applications, documenting what works, what doesn't, and what I learn along the way.

## The 100-Day Strategy

### Phase 1 — Foundations | Days 1–10
Understand the building blocks of modern AI applications: LLMs, tokens, context, prompting, APIs, structured outputs, embeddings, tool calling and evaluation.

### Phase 2 — Document Intelligence | Days 11–30
Explore how AI can turn unstructured business documents into structured, validated data.

Projects:
- Invoice → structured fields
- Waste destination sheet → material docket upload

### Phase 3 — Sustainability Intelligence | Days 31–50
Build an AI-assisted system that suggests sustainability classifications for vendor pay items.

Focus:
- Classification
- Context
- Confidence
- Human review
- Evaluation

### Phase 4 — Entity Resolution | Days 51–70
Explore how AI can identify the correct plant/equipment record from imperfect user input.

Potential identifiers include plant name, Asset ID, LV number, PPID and registration number.

### Phase 5 — Plant Forever | Days 71–90
Build a prototype for plantation programs where a photograph and GPS location create a persistent digital record of an individual plant.

Explore how the idea could grow into long-term plantation tracking and verification.

### Phase 6 — Consolidation | Days 91–100
Bring the lessons together: architecture, evaluation, security, cost, human-in-the-loop design and production considerations.

Finish with working demonstrations and a retrospective.
